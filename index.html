MODEL ECOSYSTEM

Build a meadow. Change one condition. Trace what happens.

Sunlight and water support producers. Herbivores depend on producer food. A predator depends on animal prey. This is a simplified model, not a prediction for a real ecosystem.

About 10–15 minutes. Model values run from 0 to 100 and are not scientific measurements.

      MODEL SUMMARY

      Relationships make changes travel through a food web.

      In this model, low light or water changes producer growth first. Producer food then affects herbivores, and prey levels affect the fox. Real ecosystems contain many more species, feedbacks, delays and physical conditions.

      Next practice: draw a local food web and label which arrows show energy flow and which conditions are nonliving resources.
